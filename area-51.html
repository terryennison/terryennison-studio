Area 51

Nightlife and event brand

Creative Director, brand and event direction

Area 51 is a nightlife and event brand I co-ran in Accra, built as a surreal world for its community, the Slimes. The alien green and the Slimes language carried from the flyers into the room, across nights like Slimey Saturdays and FEIN. I set the concept and directed the look and feel of every night.
